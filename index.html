How do I calculate GST on an amount?

Enter the amount before GST, select the applicable GST rate and press “Calculate GST”. The calculator shows the tax amount and total including GST.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Enter the percentage and the number, then select “Calculate Percentage”. The result is the percentage value of that number.

How can I find the price after a discount?

Enter the original price and discount percentage. The calculator displays the discount amount and final price.
